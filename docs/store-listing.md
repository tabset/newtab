## Detailed Description

```
NewTab replaces your default new tab page with a clean, elegant workspace that stays out of your way — until you need it.

──────────────────────────────────────
🚀 DOCK
──────────────────────────────────────
A macOS-style Dock sits at the edge of your screen, ready to launch your favorite apps and bookmarks in one click.

• Place it at the bottom, top, left, or right
• Smooth magnification effect on hover
• Drag to reorder items
• Right-click context menu for quick actions


──────────────────────────────────────
🔖 BOOKMARK MANAGER
──────────────────────────────────────
A full-featured bookmark system built right into your new tab — no more digging through the browser menu.

• 4 icon types: website favicon, SVG, Emoji, or plain text
• Custom icon background color (solid or gradient)
• Organize bookmarks into categories
• Grid view or list view — your choice
• Bulk mode: select multiple, delete or add to Dock at once
• Press Cmd/Ctrl + D on any webpage to instantly save it
  → Already saved? A toast notification will let you know — no duplicate dialogs
• The bookmark dialog appears directly on the page, no new tab needed


──────────────────────────────────────
🖼️ WALLPAPER SYSTEM
──────────────────────────────────────
Every new tab can look different. Set a mood, or let it surprise you.

• Solid color, gradient, or image backgrounds
• Load images from a URL or an API source
• Adjust blur and opacity in real time
• Auto-rotate on a schedule: every 5 minutes, 30 minutes, hourly, daily, or monthly
• Smart caching keeps tab loads instant


──────────────────────────────────────
🔍 SEARCH
──────────────────────────────────────
Search the web or your bookmarks from a single bar.

• 9 built-in engines: Google, Bing, Yahoo, Baidu, DuckDuckGo, Ask, GitHub, Yandex, Naver
• Filter your local bookmarks in real time as you type
• Switch between local and online search with one click


──────────────────────────────────────
🌐 6 LANGUAGES
──────────────────────────────────────
English · 简体中文 · 繁體中文 · 日本語 · 한국어 · Русский


──────────────────────────────────────
🔒 PRIVACY
──────────────────────────────────────
All your data is stored locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any external server.
NewTab does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.


──────────────────────────────────────
⌨️ KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
──────────────────────────────────────
Cmd/Ctrl + D   →  Bookmark the current page (from any website)
Cmd/Ctrl + O   →  Open the add-bookmark dialog (from the new tab page)
Escape         →  Close any panel or dialog
```
